# Priveria

**Name of Tool:** Priveria

**Primary Focus Area (select one):** Privacy Risk Assessment

**Brief Description:** Priveria is an open-source privacy governance platform that turns Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) into continuous, reusable, threat-informed assessments. It provides a guided DPIA wizard, a transparent and configurable risk-scoring calculator, and contextual privacy threat modeling (LINDDUN, MAESTRO, STRIDE, and MITRE ATLAS) adapted for AI and agentic systems. Assessment outputs map to GDPR Article 35, the EU AI Act, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, and ISO/IEC 42001 and 42005, and can be exported as regulator-, board-, and engineering-ready reports or as machine-readable policy-as-code.

**Additional Notes:** Apache-2.0 licensed and built for privacy engineers, Data Protection Officers, and AI governance teams. The project reframes the DPIA from a one-time legal artifact into a living object that re-evaluates when underlying data flows, model versions, vendors, or permissions change. Community discussions and a feedback survey are available in the repository.
